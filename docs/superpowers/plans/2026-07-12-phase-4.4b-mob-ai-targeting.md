## Goal

Give hostile physical NPCs a real combat behavior loop while preserving Phase 4.4A safe-zone protection.

This phase starts with in-room targeting, attacks, and one-room pursuit. Elite RED-alert spawning and body-guarding are later vertical slices.

---

## Slice 1 — In-Room Physical Mob AI — COMPLETE

Implemented:

- `MobAiSystem` heartbeat subscriber.
- Hostile NPCs with `AiComponent.state = 'hostile'` can select valid player targets in the same physical room.
- Effective safe zones no-op automated hostile mob attacks.
- Jacked-in deckers are targetable through `DeckerComponent.physicalRoomId` instead of their matrix `PositionComponent.roomId`.
- Mob attacks resolve through the existing `MoveDispatcher` and `AttackExecutor`.
- Reinforcement and mission-target mobs are created with hostile AI state so production-spawned hostile NPCs participate in the loop.
- AP-starved hostile mobs enter recovery so `CombatTickSystem` can refill AP and allow later attacks.
- Server composition subscribes `MobAiSystem` with `WorldService` as the safe-zone policy.
- Shared `SafeZonePolicy` contract lives in the World domain and is consumed by alarm, reinforcement, and mob AI systems.

Deferred:

- Room-to-room chase/follow behavior.
- Safe-zone boundary drop behavior during pursuit.
- Body-guarding/interception for jacked-in deckers.
- RED-alert elite mob spawning.
- Combat log/broadcast output for autonomous NPC attacks.

---

## Slice 2 — One-Room Pursuit and Safe-Zone Boundary Drop — COMPLETE

Implemented:

- Hostile mobs retain `AiComponent.targetEntityId` between ticks.
- If a target leaves the mob's room and is in an adjacent non-safe room, the mob moves one room toward the target.
- If the target's destination room is an effective safe zone, the mob drops the target and does not cross the boundary.
- Pursuit uses room exits through the World service lookup and does not attack on the same tick as movement.
- Physical movement and navigation handlers sync active player ECS `PositionComponent` values after successful WorldService movement so pursuit observes real runtime movement.

Deferred:

- Multi-room pathfinding.
- Patrol route expansion for YELLOW/RED alert.
- Combat log/broadcast output for mob movement.
